Yorki Staff Guide

Quick guide for staff members who submit requests and manage the tasks assigned to them.

Staff access: Staff members can submit new tasks and view tasks assigned to them. They can also update details on tasks they own or are assigned to.

How To Submit A New Task

  1. Sign in to Yorki.
  2. Open Submit.
  3. Enter a short, clear task title.
  4. Add the description and any job details.
  5. Set a priority level.
  6. If needed, choose the person the task should be assigned to.
  7. Add a due date if the task must happen on a specific day or time.
  8. Add the address if the work is tied to a location.
  9. Add checklist items if the task has multiple parts.
  10. Attach files or photos if they help explain the work.
  11. Click Submit.

How To Work With An Assigned Task

  1. Open Dashboard.
  2. Click the task card to open the task details.
  3. Read the description, due date, checklist, comments, and attachments.
  4. Add comments when you need to share progress or ask questions.
  5. If allowed, update the checklist or task details as work progresses.

What Staff Can Expect

  • If a task is assigned to you, it appears on your board.
  • If a task is not assigned to you, it usually will not appear on your staff board.
  • Admins may reassign tasks, change priority, move tasks, archive them, or complete them.

Good Staff Habits

  • Use comments to document important updates.
  • Keep the checklist current so everyone knows progress.
  • Attach photos or files when they help clarify the work.
  • Use a due date only when timing actually matters.
